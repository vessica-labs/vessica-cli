# Account and topic scope

Use this file to classify relevance. It does not authorize account actions or
change commercial forecasts.

## Active accounts

Prioritize mail that materially affects:

| Canonical account | Safe aliases | Internal relationship context |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Agilent | Agilent Technologies | AI Transformation program |
| Mastercard | MasterCard | PDLC Next; Haytham leads delivery |
| Pacific Life | PacificLife, PacficLife | AI Transformation program |
| RBC | Royal Bank of Canada | PDLC Next; Becky leads delivery |
| Western Digital | WDC | AI builds and AI Transformation |
| Qualcomm | Qualcomm Incorporated | AI Transformation; Justin Jaison (JJ) is CCO |
| Micron | Micron Technology | AI Transformation; Ramiro Palmo is CCO |

An alias is a clue, not proof. A common abbreviation such as “MA” or “WD” is too
ambiguous to establish an account match by itself. Do not map subsidiaries or
spun-off businesses without explicit evidence in the thread or directory data.

## Lower-touch accounts

Include meaningful developments from Cisco, AWS/Amazon Web Services, TD Bank,
and CIBC. Treat these as lower priority unless the message contains a direct
request, decision, opportunity, commitment, or escalation.

## Priority topics

Include useful mail about:

- Enterprise AI implementation and transformation.
- Large-scale AI product-development lifecycle change.
- Material releases of foundation models or enterprise AI platforms.
- Evidence relevant to BCG being recognized as a leading AI consultancy.
- High-value material for the user’s AI thought leadership.

Do not broaden this into generic technology, management, finance, or world-news
coverage. A newsletter belongs in the batch only when it contains a concise,
specific insight relevant to one of the topics above.

## Relationship classification

- `client`: a person employed by or directly representing a client account.
- `colleague`: an internal colleague, including account and delivery leaders.
- `external_partner`: an analyst, platform partner, board/ELT contact, publisher,
  or other relevant external stakeholder.
- `unknown`: insufficient evidence.

Record an employer or title only when it appears in trusted connector metadata,
the person’s signature, or an unambiguous message statement. Mark those fields
as explicit. Never invent a title from tone or presumed seniority.
